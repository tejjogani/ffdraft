# 13 — The jewelry CAD feature set: the incumbents, a capability matrix, frequency of use, and the gaps in the freeform plan

Research date: 2026-11-25. Scope: what jewelers actually get from Rhino and from each jewelry CAD product on sale today (named tools, status, price), how often a working designer uses each job, and what is missing from [`docs/plans/freeform-cad.md`](../plans/freeform-cad.md) and from the product. Builds on [`12-rhino-parity.md`](./12-rhino-parity.md) (the Rhino tool inventory and engine facts, not repeated here; corrected in §7), [`01-cad.md`](./01-cad.md), [`06-freeform.md`](./06-freeform.md) and [`cad-workspace.md`](../plans/cad-workspace.md).

**How to read the evidence.** `[V]` I opened the page this session and the claim is on it; the link is beside it. `[V repo]` I read it in this repository this session. `[S]` seen only in a search result or an earlier repo file, not opened. `[E]` my estimate or working knowledge; I say what it rests on. ## 1. The short answer

- **The market moved in 2026, and three of the moves are aimed at Pavé's ground.** Rhino 9 is on sale and ships an AI assistant (RhinoAI) [V]. RhinoArtisan 7 calls itself the AI release: a local MCP server so Claude or ChatGPT can drive its jewelry tools, a built-in assistant, live diamond prices from a B2B marketplace (Nivoda), sprues and print hand-off [V]. Drakon 2 (the product panther3d.com now redirects to) runs on Rhino 9 with a "Copilot" [V]. And one browser product, Diatech Make (Dubai), already describes itself as AI-native browser jewelry CAD with an MCP endpoint, free with an account [V]. MatrixGold 4.0 (May 2026) announces no AI and is built for Rhino 8 [V]. "An assistant that operates jewelry CAD" is now a feature others ship. What nobody ships is Pavé's whole line: Design board, then CAD with checks, then renders, free, with no Rhino licence underneath.
- **`freeform-cad.md` covers the from-scratch Rhino layer well. Its gaps are in the jewelry layer and the workshop layer.** Almost everything the incumbents added in 2025–26 falls in those two layers: weight reduction (Scoop, azure cutters), prongs placed between stones and edited in groups, matching bands, a clearer tech report with a stone map, custom and scanned stones, a ring re-sizer, sprues [V]. The plan has none of these as steps.
- **The eight most important gaps, in order** (§5): (1) weight reduction: a ring scoop and azure cutters; (2) manual control inside automatic stone layouts: pin, move, lock, mirror, prongs between stones; (3) the tech sheet and stone map as a screen (the engine exists, the screen does not); (4) more stones and heads: tapered baguette, trapezoid, hexagon, custom outline, trellis, tulip, hidden halo, cluster; (5) matching and contour bands; (6) casting hand-off: shrink scaling and a single sprue at export; (7) opening and re-sizing other people's files; (8) a parts library with the user's own components.
- **One strategic weakness stays open: SubD.** Every Rhino plug-in released since 2022 ships SubD builders (CrossGems, Drakon, RhinoArtisan, 3Design's 3Shaper) and Rhino 9 extended SubD again [V]. The plan says "not planned". Neither of Pavé's engines has SubD, so I agree it cannot be in the eight steps, but it should become a named later step (a cage modeller whose output is a castable mesh), not an unscheduled footnote.
- **Reject** (§6): print supports and orientation (every incumbent hands off to the printer's own program), full casting trees, a counter-top catalogue of thousands of styles, Voronoi and filigree generators before the basics, a `.jcd` importer.
- **Corrections to earlier repo research** (§7): RhinoGold is not "now sold as RhinoArtisan"; Panther has two claimed successors; MatrixGold is at 4.0; Rhino is at 9.

### 2.2 Rhino itself, as jewelers use it

Research 12 §2–3 lists the tools and ranks them; that stands. Three additions.

- **What bare Rhino lacks for a jeweler** `[E]`, from the plug-in lists below: stones with carat weights (McNeel offers only a set of sample gem solids [V rhino3d.com/jewelry]), ring sizes, heads, cutters, weight by alloy, stone reports, and edits that survive a change of size. That list is the whole reason the plug-in market exists.
- **Rhino 9 additions that matter here** [V [new in 9](https://www.rhino3d.com/9/new/)]: Patch can fill holes with five or more sides; fillets with setbacks that carry on across tangent faces; push or pull of several faces at once; objects repeated along curves with spacing, scale and orientation controls; closed curves inflated into soft shapes; light refraction through gems and more gem materials; Grasshopper 2; RhinoAI. A trade review dated 18 November 2026 covers the same list [V [Digital Production](https://digitalproduction.com/2026/11/18/rhino-9-patches-subds-and-the-ai-in-the-room/)]. The exact release date was not on any page I opened.
- **Consequence for the plan's aliases.** The plan maps Rhino command names to Pavé verbs. Rhino 9 adds single-key shortcuts and a new command line [V new in 9]; whether any command the plan aliases was renamed is **not verified**.

Two details worth keeping, because they show how the incumbents think about micro-setting. RhinoArtisan's Automatic Pavé takes a surface, polysurface, mesh or SubD, fills from a picked point or from the border inward, uses only the stone sizes the user lists, lets the user add, move, resize, lock and delete single stones (overlaps turn red), mirrors in X, Y or both, and can add prongs and drill cutters; edge prongs are left to the user [V [doc](https://rhinoartisan.com/docs/7/diamonds/pave/automatic-pave/)]. Its Micro Setting tool does not make beads at all: it makes the cutters (U, V, square, trapezoid or French shape; V channels; prong rows) that a boolean then carves, which is how a setter's bright-cut is modeled [V [doc](https://rhinoartisan.com/docs/7/diamonds/gemsets/micro-setting/)]. How MatrixGold's Gem Springs behaves I could not open at source `[E: stones that push apart to even spacing]`.

**JewelCAD** `[E]`, working knowledge, not verified this session: curves, then single, double and triple rail surfaces, booleans, arrays, a stone and setting library, weight, and slicing with supports for wax printers. Its place in this file is its file format: a browser rival already imports `.jcd` [V cad.diatech.ai].

## 5. The gaps in `freeform-cad.md`, ranked

Ranking is by how often a jeweler meets the job, whether the plan or the product already answers it, and how hard it is on Pavé's engines. "How incumbents do it" cites §2.3.

| # | Gap | Why a jeweler cares | How incumbents do it | Feasibility on Pavé's engines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weight reduction: ring scoop and azure cutters** | Metal is priced by the gram; a heavy model loses the quote. MatrixGold 4.0 leads with it | Scoop: pick the ring, set a wall; azures: a tapered window under each stone, leaving ribs | **Scoop: needs work, days.** On `ringShank` and ring-rail sweeps the sections are known, so the cutter is a second sweep with each profile inset by the wall, limited to an arc and faded at its ends; the plan already found profile inset to be the robust way to hollow [V plan §4.4]. **Azures: needs work.** One lofted cutter per stone from the drill hole to a larger rounded square on the inner surface; Manifold took 2,000 cutters in 1.6 s [V research 12]. Clear on rings and shells. On a solid sculpted body "the inside" is undefined: offer only where metal behind the stone ends within a few mm. Not judged: exact booleans with hundreds of lofted cutters for STEP; expect mesh at production as seats are today |
| 2 | **Hand control inside automatic layouts** | No setter accepts a fully automatic field: stones near an edge, a corner or a motif get moved by hand | Auto fill, then add, move, lock, delete; mirror; prongs placed between neighbours; edit many prongs at once | **Needs work, no engine risk.** The layout is Pavé's own TypeScript (`mesh/pave/layout.ts`), already deterministic [V repo]. Add keyed pins to `pave` and rows (`{at, size, locked}`, removed spots, a mirror plane) and let the solver treat pins as fixed. Keyed records match the plan's point convention, so a designer's pin and an assistant's edit undo separately. The hard part is interaction, and stable identity for stones that are *not* pinned (they have none; say so in the UI) |
| 3 | **Tech sheet and stone map on screen** | The sheet goes to the workshop and the client every time | Two-page report with a colour-coded stone map, lists to Excel, cost breakdown | **Native: it exists.** `packages/production` already makes views, dimensions, stone call-outs, `stones.csv`, a cost model and a PDF, from scripts only [V repo, HANDOVER §3]. Missing: a screen, a colour-by-size view of stones in the viewport, live diamond prices (the sourcing work). The plan lists Make2D as "Later" without saying this engine exists |
| 4 | **More stones and heads** | Bridal work is variety on a few themes; side stones are often shapes Pavé lacks `[E]` | New cuts each release; custom gem from outline to culet; scanned stone converter; trellis, tulip, martini, peg heads; hidden halo; cluster; toi et moi | **Cuts: native.** Step cuts on a convex outline (tapered baguette, trapezoid, hexagon, lozenge, half moon, kite) are data for the existing facet generator. **Custom gem:** a drawn outline (step 1 curve) plus crown and pavilion heights for step style; a custom brilliant is harder, not judged. **Scanned stone:** a mesh as the stone, its girdle outline for the seat; fits `curveOp{outline: gem}` in step 3. **Heads:** each style is a new arrangement of sweeps in `setting.ts`, days each. **Hidden halo:** a halo whose stones face outward below the girdle: a new placement mode |
| 5 | **Matching and contour bands** | Sold with most engagement rings; named as a core duty in the advert | Take the ring's outline at finger level, offset it by a fit gap, build a band on that rail | **Needs work, after step 3.** Pieces exist: a body's top-view outline (`mesh/outline`), 2D offset (Manifold `CrossSection.offset` [V research 12]), a sweep with centre and width laws (step 3). Open design question, not an engine one: two rings in one design, or a second piece linked to the first |
| 6 | **Casting hand-off: shrink scaling, a sprue** | In-house casters and mould makers scale every master; bureaus do it themselves [S research 02] | RhinoArtisan: 12 sprue layouts, copies on a plate, an engraved job mark; JCD: sprue creator [V]. No incumbent page I opened shows a shrink tool; designers scale by hand `[E]` | **Native.** Shrink is one uniform scale at export, driven by the caster profile; `METALS[..].shrinkagePct` and the manifest's flag are already there [V repo]. The plan cuts it; it costs a day. One sprue (a tapered rod on the thickest section, added only to the casting file) is a small node. Trees: reject (§6) |
| 7 | **Open and re-size other people's files** | Changing existing files for size and repairing vendor files are in the job description (§4) | Ring re-sizer on any model; estimate size from geometry; one-pass STL repair; importers that recover stones from rival formats | **Mixed.** Estimate size: fit a circle to the bore of a mesh, simple. Re-size a mesh ring: a Manifold `warp` that changes the radius only in the lower arc and leaves the head rigid (15 ms warps measured [V research 12]); honest limits about ±2 sizes and thickness drift `[E]`. STEP bodies are whole-body only in the plan, so a re-sized import is a mesh with the badge. 3DM import is "Later" in the plan; rhino3dm reads curves, meshes and layers but has no mesher for NURBS solids `[E]`, so quality depends on meshes saved in the file. **I cannot judge this without sample vendor files**, and the files that must stay out of the repo cannot be used |
| 8 | **Own parts library** | Shops reuse their heads, shanks and findings; it is how house style is kept | Library, user elements, import and export of elements, profile libraries, thousands of counter styles | **Needs work, no engine risk.** `cloneNodes` (plan §2) already copies a subtree with fresh ids. A part is a saved subtree with named inputs (host, stone, size). Useful to the assistant too: "use our six-claw head" |
| 9 | **Text along a curve; class and award ring tops** | Name pieces, signet legends, class rings | Text on curve; award ring builder with text, artwork and fluted bezels | **Needs work, small, after step 1.** Glyph outlines exist; place each glyph on a frame along a drawn curve, then engrave or emboss on the host as today |
| 10 | **Curves drawn on a body** | Borders of a stone field, bright-cut lines, milgrain on a dome | Curve on Object (MatrixGold 4.0); geodesic and offset curves on an object (3Design V13) | **Needs work.** The plan defers Project and Pull because OCCT's projection classes are absent. A lighter route is enough: click points snap to the body (BVH hit), the curve is the interpolation re-projected by closest point (`ProjectPointOnSurf` is in the build [V research 12]). Unlocks region borders for row 5 and gap 2 |
| 11 | **Instances on stones** | Custom prongs, custom cutters or ornaments repeated at every stone | Copy to gems; prongs on gem; create and link "positions" | **Native, small.** A `pattern` type that takes its frames from a row, halo or field. A good primitive for the assistant |
| 12 | **Thickness map, balance check, live stone gaps** | Seeing thin metal beats reading a warning; a top-heavy ring spins on the finger | Global Thickness map, Balancer Checker, real-time distances between stones, overlaps in red | **Native.** Wall readings by ray cast already exist (`dfm/wallProbe.ts`, `production/dfm/thickness.ts` [V repo]); colour the mesh with them at production detail. Balance: centre of mass of metal plus stones against the finger axis |
| 13 | **Hinges, bangles, hoops, links** | Earrings and bracelets are a large part of most lines `[E]`; hinges, clasps and linkages are in the advert (§4) | Hinge, bangle, hoop and link builders | **Needs work per part.** Simple solids; the care is in clearances between moving parts and in keeping them separate shells, which the checks already report (`metal.separate`) |
| 14 | **SubD cage modeling** | Organic shanks, bombé and signet forms; every new plug-in has it | SubD shank, signet, ring and bezel starters; convert to NURBS | **Research for exact, large for mesh.** No SubD in either engine. A cage modeller with Catmull–Clark in TypeScript whose result is a castable mesh is weeks of interaction work; I cannot size it closer. STEP would carry it only as a mesh |
| 15 | **Surface textures and pierced patterns** | Hammered and honeycomb finishes for fashion lines | Texture 3D, honeycomb, Voronoi, filigree from an image | **Mesh only**; needs a surface parameterisation, which sweeps have and sculpts do not. Low priority |

Three things the plan already has right and the incumbents confirm: editable history everywhere (the headline of every product in §2.3); stones on any surface including meshes; and rounding built into heads and profiles rather than filleted afterwards (RhinoArtisan lists "Cap Round" and "Smart Profiles" among its solid tools [V docs]).

## 6. What this means for the plan

Inputs for `docs/plans/freeform-cad-additions.md`, in priority order.

**A. Changes to the eight steps (cheap while the step is open).**
1. *Step 3:* when stones publish snap points, also publish a per-stone frame list, so gap 11 (`pattern` on stones) is one small addition in step 4.
2. *Step 6:* add pins to `pave` and to `gemRow.along` (gap 2: add, move, lock, remove, mirror), text along a curve (gap 9), and "draw on body" curves (gap 10) so a stone field can have a drawn border. Step 6 is then the micro-setting step, and should be tested against the RhinoArtisan behaviours listed in §2.3.
3. *Step 8:* restore shrink scaling as an export option tied to the caster profile (gap 6), add the thickness map and balance check (gap 12), and put the existing tech sheet behind a screen with a colour-by-size stone view (gap 3). Remove "Make2D drawings" and "shrinkage compensation" from the plan's scope cuts or explain them.

**B. New steps after step 8, in order.**
9. **Weight**: ring scoop on `ringShank` and ring-rail sweeps; azure cutters for rows on rings, then for fields on shells; a target-weight readout ("3.9 g, target 3.5 g"). Proof: wall never under the profile's minimum; weight falls by the predicted amount; "Ready to cast" still holds.
10. **Stones and heads II**: six step-cut shapes, custom gem from an outline, mesh stones, trellis, tulip, martini and peg heads, hidden halo, cluster and toi-et-moi layouts.
11. **Sets**: matching and contour bands, which needs the decision on two rings in one design.
12. **Parts**: the own-parts library, then hinge, bangle and hoop as the first shipped parts.
13. **Bring-in**: estimate size, mesh ring re-sizer, 3DM import as meshes, curves and layers.
14. **SubD cage as a castable mesh**, with four starters (shank, signet, bezel, ring). Only after 9–13.

**C. Reject, and why.**
- **Print supports and orientation.** RhinoArtisan, the most complete workshop tool found, does not generate supports: it sends the plate to the printer's own program [V manufacturing tab]. Pro-J has a support tool [V]; no other product whose pages I opened shows one.
- **Casting trees** (helix, spiral, cluster). A production-floor job for whoever owns the flask, not a design job; one sprue is enough.
- **A catalogue of thousands of styles.** CounterSketch's value is Stuller making the piece [V]. Pavé's answer is templates plus the assistant.
- **Voronoi, Jali and filigree generators** before gaps 1–8.
- **A `.jcd` importer.** Proprietary format; I could not even open the vendor's site. Revisit only if the founder's customers ask.
- **Rhino's own drafting** (layouts, GD&T). The production sheet covers the workshop's need.

**D. Open questions for the founder.**
1. Who is the first paying user: a custom jeweler who sends files to a casting bureau, or a manufacturer who casts in-house? It decides whether gap 6 (shrink, sprue) is in the first release.
2. Is a "set" (engagement ring plus matching band) one design with two rings or two linked pieces?
3. RhinoArtisan 7 quotes live diamond prices inside CAD from a B2B marketplace and exposes its tools over MCP [V]. Does Pavé want to answer that directly (the partner API for the founder's own diamond marketplace, and Pavé's MCP server) at launch?
4. Is SubD worth a later step of several weeks, or is "import a sculpt and make it castable" enough for the first year?
5. Should the plan's wording move from "Rhino 8" to "Rhino 9" now that 9 is the version on sale?
