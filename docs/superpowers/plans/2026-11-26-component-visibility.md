# Picking and viewing jewelry components

Clicking a stone in a generated row selects that stone on the first click. Hide and Isolate act on its evaluated identity. Selecting the row in Features continues to address the entire feature. A metal click addresses the individual metal object that supplied the hit.

- Keep hidden features, stone instances, metal parts and isolation in project-scoped view state. Preserve earlier hides across isolation, support a visible exit and reset the view state on project switches.
- Apply visibility in the renderer, picking, outlines, fit and guide overlays. A body under a construction guide gets the first hit. Give the CAD view a neutral studio backdrop with a darker option.
- Collapse construction curves and stone lists in Features. Expose direct view actions for the current selection.
- Verify through a browser using a generated pavé ring and actual imported bodies in the open project. Check saved design and export separately.
