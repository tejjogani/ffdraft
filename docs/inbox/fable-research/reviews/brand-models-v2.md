# Review of docs/plans/brand-models-v2.md

Verdict: ready after fixes. Steps 1 to 3 (no AI) can start once findings 8 and 9 are fixed. No step that calls a provider with a person in the picture should start until the safety design (findings 1 to 5) is fixed: the age gate as written either kills the ring flow or is not a real gate.

Labels: `[V]` opened at source today (25 Nov 2026), `[S]` search result only, `[E]` my estimate. "Code" means the read-only export at `/home/claude/ref/assistant-build`.

## Suspicions, not verified

- **Blender holdout and reflections.** The plan needs the proxy to be a holdout for the camera and still reflect in the metal. I believe Cycles object holdout affects camera rays only, but did not run it. The worker today has no holdout, shadow or stone-id pass (`render.py` renders beauty on a transparent film, then a mask with everything else hidden, which ignores occlusion by a proxy).
- **Registration by translation only.** Instruction editors often rescale or re-crop the whole frame slightly. A translation search will not recover that; the overlap check would then fail good plates and acceptance would sit well under the guessed 65%.
- **"Multiply shadow where the plate painted none."** No method is given for telling a painted shadow from skin shading. Double shadows are likely.
- **Will the Claude judge answer apparent-age questions reliably, or hedge?** Not testable here (no key). It bears directly on finding 1.
- **Hosted mode and US child-safety reporting duties.** A hosted service that learns of apparent child sexual abuse material may have reporting and preservation duties that conflict with "the image is never stored". For counsel before hosting; not researched.
- **Amazon and Etsy rules** rest on news articles in the research, not the platforms' own pages. Not re-opened.
- **SynthID after compositing.** Whether the provider's watermark survives in the unpasted area after re-encode was not checked; the plan's `providerMarks.synthId` field may record a mark that is no longer detectable.
- **An "EU icon".** I did not find or check a final EU-wide icon or its terms of use.
- **Kling 2.6 at $0.07 a second on Higgsfield's newer API** was not re-opened; the older platform page (opened) lists no Kling.
