# Review of docs/plans/freeform-cad-additions.md

Verdict: **ready after fixes for steps 9 to 18; not ready from step 19 on.** The licence duties of the custom engine build are in no step, the build rests on wrong toolchain facts, and three contracts (general NURBS, SubD to NURBS, job-made geometry) have holes that would stall steps 20 to 28.

Counts: 1 blocker, 13 majors, 13 minors.

Labels: `[V]` opened or run today (25 Nov 2026), `[S]` search summary, `[E]` my estimate. "Shipped build" means `replicad-opencascadejs@1.2.1` under `/home/claude/bugsweep/kernel/node_modules/.pnpm/`. "Plan" means `freeform-cad-additions.md`. "R22", "R13" are the research files.

## Suspicions, not verified

- **QuadriFlow upkeep.** The repository page showed no commit dates; I believe the last real work is several years old `[E]`. Check before adopting.
- **OpenSubdiv in WebAssembly.** Its core has no dependencies, so it should compile; no one documents it (the plan says so). P5 decides.
- **occ_gordon on OpenCascade 8.** It comes from a code base built against version 7. OCCT 8 renamed its collection types (the shipped typings show `NCollection_Array1_gp_Pnt` and no `TColgp_*`). It may need porting. P8 decides.
- **openNURBS licence wording.** The repository page links a LICENSE file whose text I could not read. The tree bundles `zlib` and `freetype263`; FreeType has its own credit clause if it is compiled in. The plan's "MIT [V this session]" is verified by me only for rhino3dm (its `package.json`).

- **Patents.** I know of no live patent on Catmull–Clark creases or on field-aligned quad meshing `[E]`; not searched.
- **Azure cutters and stone security.** Nothing says an azure's opening must stay smaller than the stone's seat. "Ribs kept" may cover it.
