K / DEF$1 each. Do not spend meaningful capital here.
BenchPrefer upside RB/WR and possibly QB3 over safe low-ceiling veterans.
Budget floorAlways keep at least $1 for every empty roster slot.

8 Nomination strategy

Draft phaseNominateWhy
First ~25%Popular expensive stars you do NOT need$1 nomination. Forces competitors to reveal willingness to pay and drains budgets if they overbid.
MiddlePositions your opponents still needCan trigger bidding wars and improve later prices at the positions you actually want.
When you see a bargain tierA player you want$1 nomination. If demand is weak, you can capture him cheaply.
LateYour actual targetsBudgets are constrained. Know every opponent’s max possible bid.
Opening price: default opening nomination = $1. The opening price is not your valuation — starting at $1 maximizes information and lets the room set the price. Only open higher late if you know the room cannot meaningfully contest it.

11 Final 60-second checklist

  • Open the Yahoo draft room 10–15 minutes early.
  • Keep this guide open beside it.
  • Plan for two starting QBs — but don’t panic-buy the first two nominated.
  • Nominate $1 early. Use stars you can happily let others overpay for.
  • Before every bid, look at the Max column.
  • If a player goes above Max and alternatives remain: STOP.
  • Watch remaining QB supply and teams still needing QB2.
  • Watch opponents’ remaining cash — late buying power is leverage.
  • $1 kicker. $1 defense.
  • Use nearly all $200 by the end.
  • Send the live format above whenever the room changes materially.
